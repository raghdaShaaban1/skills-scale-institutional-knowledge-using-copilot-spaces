# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project management approach with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and data-informed decisions. The lifecycle moves from initiation and stakeholder alignment through planning and execution, release, retrospective, and continuous improvement.

## Project Management Process Summary

- **Initiation:** Validate the business need, define goals and success metrics, align stakeholders, identify risks and resource needs, and make a go/no-go decision.
- **Planning:** Convert approved initiatives into a prioritized and estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, release plans, and dependency tracking.
- **Execution and tracking:** Manage delivery through a project board, small pull requests, regular standups and delivery syncs, testing, code review, quality checks, and progress metrics.
- **Risk management and communication:** Maintain a risk register, monitor dependencies, provide regular stakeholder updates, and escalate blockers through the defined PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor path.
- **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria and CI/security checks, prepare release notes and rollback plans, deploy through staging and production verification, and communicate releases to stakeholders and support.
- **Retrospectives and continuous improvement:** Review what went well and what could improve, assign focused action items with owners and due dates, track outcomes, and incorporate learnings into future work.
- **Roles and personas:** Clarify responsibilities and collaboration expectations for Developers, Product Managers, Project Managers, QA/Testing, and stakeholders.
